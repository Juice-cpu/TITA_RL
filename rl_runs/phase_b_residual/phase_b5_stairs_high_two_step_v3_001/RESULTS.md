# Phase B5 HIGH two-step v3 results

Date: 2026-09-13

## Configuration

- 64 vector environments, 192 rollout steps, 3 updates
- HIGH posture, command speed 0.15 m/s
- 2 steps, height 0.005-0.015 m, tread 0.60-0.75 m, width 1.00-1.30 m
- frozen official base policy and frozen policy log standard deviation (-3.8)
- learning rate 1e-7
- warm start: flat selected residual
- fixed offline P95: route 5.4187655, energy 2.5330608, mode 0.6782783,
  smooth 0.2076107

## Training rollouts

The rollout at iteration N was generated by checkpoint N-1.

| Rollout | Policy checkpoint | Strict completions | Hard failures | Verified-level histogram |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | [3, 23, 38] |
| 2 | 1 | 2 | 5 collision | [7, 6, 51] |
| 3 | 2 | 0 | 1 collision | [58, 4, 2] |

The original training script reported cumulative success counters, so rollout 3
was initially misread as having two new completions. The counter was unchanged
from rollout 2; therefore rollout 3 had zero new strict completions. The script
now snapshots the counter before every rollout and reports only new successes.

The stair-specific P95 removed the erroneous route-cost saturation. Normalized
route cost was 0.046 in rollout 2 instead of approximately 6.99 in the prior
flat-P95 experiment.

Neither candidate met the gate of at least 2 completions and at most 4 hard

## Conclusion

Correct cost normalization is necessary but was not sufficient. Stochastic PPO
rollouts found successful trajectories, while the deterministic policy mean did
not retain them. Successful-trajectory collection subsequently produced 2
episodes/1003 samples at seed 163 and 8 episodes/3667 samples at seed 167. Three
actor-only distillation candidates were tested, but all produced zero strict
deterministic completions on seed 163; none was selected. This result does not
validate three-step stairs, normal stairs, domain randomization, or hardware
deployment.
